# Future Ideas

A living document of ideas, explorations, and possibilities for Life OS. These are **not commitments** — they are seeds for future thinking.

> Ideas here do not belong in Version 1 code. They are captured here so they aren't lost.

---

## Dashboard & Intelligence

- **Life Score** — a single composite score (0–100) computed from all health, finance, learning, planning, and habit data
- **Morning Briefing** — a generated daily narrative: "Here's your day: you have 3 tasks due, ₹12k spent this week, workout streak is 5 days..."
- **Evening Wind-down** — prompted review of the day: what was done, what slipped, mood capture
- **Predictive Alerts** — "You'll miss your savings goal by ₹4,000 this month at current spend rate"
- **Natural language commands** — "Add a task: call dentist tomorrow at 10am"
- **Personalised Roadmap** — AI-generated 7/30/90-day action plan based on active goals

---

## Cross-Module Intelligence

- **Workout → Mood → Productivity correlation** — surface hidden patterns across modules
- **Sleep quality → Focus mode suggestion** — if you slept 5h, Life OS proactively enables Focus Mode
- **Spending → Goals alignment** — flag when discretionary spending conflicts with savings goals
- **Meal plan → Shopping list auto-fill** — already partially in Food, could be smarter
- **German vocab review during commute** — intelligent study reminders based on calendar gaps

---

## Planner

- **Habit Tracking** — daily habits with streaks and visualisations
- **Time blocking** — drag tasks into calendar blocks, estimate durations
- **Focus Mode** — distraction-free single-task view with timer (Pomodoro / free-form)
- **Weekly Review** — structured Friday/Sunday review flow with prompts
- **Recurring tasks** — repeating tasks tied to schedules (e.g. "Pay rent every 1st")
- **Dependency chains** — Task B can't start until Task A is done

---

## Finance

- **Net Worth tracker** — assets, liabilities, monthly snapshot
- **Bill tracking** — recurring bills with due-date alerts (linked to Documents module)
- **Auto-split suggestion** — "This month you overspent on Dining by 40%"
- **Investment tracker** — basic portfolio entry (stocks, mutual funds, crypto)
- **Tax estimation** — income categorisation for basic tax awareness

## Health

- **Menstrual cycle tracking** — cycle prediction and health correlation
- **Heart rate / step data import** — from Apple Health, Google Fit, Garmin
- **Doctor appointments** — calendar integration + document storage for prescriptions
- **Supplement tracker** — what you take, when, and reminders
