# Future Ideas — Version 3 and Beyond

Ideas that belong to Life OS Stage 3 (Life Intelligence Platform) and onward. Too big for the current roadmap, too important to lose.

---

## Stage 3 — Life Intelligence Platform

- **Cross-domain correlations** — automatically find relationships between sleep, spending, learning, and mood across all modules
- **Proactive life audits** — "It's been 6 months since you reviewed your savings goal. Here's what changed."
- **Life benchmarking** — opt-in comparison: "People with similar income who save 20% more tend to do X differently"
- **Anomaly detection** — "This month's spending pattern is unlike any previous month — did something change?"
- **Burnout prediction** — based on task volume, sleep quality, and workout patterns, surface a burnout risk score

## Stage 4 — AI Life Companion

- **Morning system** — wake up to a briefing, not a notification flood
- **Natural language everything** — "What's my net worth?", "Have I worked out this week?", "What's my next deadline?"
- **Contextual nudges** — the right prompt at the right moment (not push notifications, something smarter)
- **Conversation-based goal setting** — "I want to learn Spanish" → Life OS builds the plan, adds vocab, creates study sessions, adds to Learning module

## Stage 5 — Life Ecosystem

- **Developer API** — third parties can build Life OS integrations
- **Widget marketplace** — community-built dashboard widgets
- **Life OS for enterprises** — team-level Life OS (not just households) for productivity-focused companies
- **Open data format** — export your entire life history in a portable, open format
- **Life OS SDK** — build apps on top of Life OS's data layer

## Long-Term Principles

These must survive into Stage 5:
1. The user's data is always theirs
2. Intelligence never becomes surveillance
3. The personal OS layer always exists — even inside an ecosystem
