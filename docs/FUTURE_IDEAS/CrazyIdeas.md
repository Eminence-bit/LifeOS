# Future Ideas — Crazy Ideas

Low probability, high imagination. These belong here so they don't clutter the serious roadmap.

> If one of these starts looking realistic, move it to `Modules.md` or `Integrations.md`.

---

- **Life OS as a local LLM** — run a small model locally, completely private, no API key needed
- **Life Replay (annual)** — your entire year as a visual timeline: workouts, spending, learning, milestones
- **Digital Twin** — an AI model trained on your own Life OS data that can simulate "what would happen if I saved 10% more?" or "what mood am I likely in next Friday based on sleep and workout patterns?"
- **Life OS for kids** — a simplified view for children (chores, homework, habits, savings jar) managed by a parent Space
- **Ambient display** — a minimal always-on view for a spare screen or e-ink display: today's tasks, weather, quote, Life Pulse
- **Smart home integration** — Life OS triggers home automations based on events ("Going to sleep" → dim lights, start sleep log)
- **Memory capture** — photo + voice note saved to a timeline, searchable years later ("what was I doing in April 2026?")
- **Life OS social layer** — opt-in sharing of achievements: "I've worked out 30 days in a row" → shared to a friend circle, not social media
- **AR overlay** — point your phone at your fridge, see what's low in inventory and what meal you can make
