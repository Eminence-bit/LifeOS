# Future Ideas — AI Intelligence

Ideas for deepening AI throughout Life OS. These are explorations, not commitments.

> See `PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md` before committing any of these. Each must satisfy one of the 7 principles.

---

## Dashboard Intelligence

- **Life Score** — a single composite 0–100 score computed from health, finance, learning, planning, and habit data
- **Morning Briefing** — a generated daily narrative: "Here's your day: 3 tasks due, ₹12k spent this week, workout streak is 5 days..."
- **Evening Wind-down** — prompted review: what was done, what slipped, mood capture
- **Predictive Alerts** — "You'll miss your savings goal by ₹4,000 this month at current spend rate"
- **Natural language commands** — "Add a task: call dentist tomorrow at 10am"
- **Personalised Roadmap** — AI-generated 7/30/90-day action plan based on active goals

## Cross-Module Pattern Recognition

- **Workout → Mood → Productivity correlation** — surface hidden patterns across modules
- **Sleep quality → Focus mode suggestion** — low sleep proactively enables Focus Mode
- **Spending vs Goals alignment** — flag discretionary spend that conflicts with savings goals
- **German vocab review timing** — suggest study windows based on calendar gaps

## Per-Module AI

- **Finance:** "Your subscription spending increased 23% this month — here are 3 you haven't used"
- **Food:** "What can I cook with what I have?" — recipe suggestion from current inventory
- **Health:** "Your body fat has increased 2% over 3 months despite daily workouts — consider reviewing nutrition"
- **Learning:** Spaced repetition scheduling, weak area detection
- **Career:** Resume gap analysis, skill recommendations based on job descriptions saved

## AI Layer Principles

- AI should feel **invisible** — insights appear in context, never in a dedicated AI panel
- AI output should be **actionable** — not "you exercised less this week" but "add a workout to Tuesday?"
- AI should never be wrong confidently — show uncertainty when data is thin
