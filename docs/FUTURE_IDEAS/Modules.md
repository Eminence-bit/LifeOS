# Future Ideas — Module Enhancements

Near-term evolution ideas for existing modules. More grounded than Crazy Ideas; more distant than the current roadmap.

---

## Planner

- **Habit Tracking** — daily habits with streaks and visualisations
- **Time blocking** — drag tasks into calendar blocks, estimate durations
- **Focus Mode** — distraction-free single-task view with timer (Pomodoro / free-form)
- **Weekly Review** — structured review flow with prompts
- **Recurring tasks** — "Pay rent every 1st", "Review budget every Sunday"
- **Dependency chains** — Task B can't start until Task A is done

## Finance

- **Net Worth tracker** — assets, liabilities, monthly snapshot
- **Bill tracker** — recurring bills with due-date alerts (linked to Documents)
- **Investment tracker** — basic portfolio entry (stocks, mutual funds, crypto)
- **Auto-split suggestion** — "This month you overspent on Dining by 40%"
- **Tax estimation** — income categorisation for basic tax awareness

## Health

- **Mood tracking** — daily mood log (1–5, emoji, notes)
- **Symptom log** — ad hoc health event capture
- **Menstrual cycle tracking** — cycle prediction and health correlation
- **Doctor appointments** — calendar + document storage for prescriptions
- **Supplement tracker** — what you take, when, reminders

## Food

- **Nutritional goals** — set macro targets, track daily achievement
- **Grocery price tracking** — which store is cheapest for which item
- **Recipe AI** — "What can I cook with what I have?"

## Learning

- **Spaced repetition engine** — proper SRS algorithm, not just review_count
- **Reading list** — books, articles, papers with notes and highlights
- **Course tracker** — links to external courses with progress logging
- **Skill graph** — visual map of skills and their relationships

## Career

- **Interview prep** — STAR method notes, per-company question bank
- **Networking log** — people met, conversations, follow-up reminders
- **Salary history** — income progression over time
- **Reference tracker** — who can vouch for you

## Documents

- **Document scanner** — camera-based scan and upload
- **Expiry push notifications** — not just in-app, but OS-level alerts
- **Template library** — cover letters, contracts, standard forms

## Second Brain

- **Bi-directional linking** — `[[Goal Name]]` syntax between notes
- **Graph view** — visual map of connected notes and ideas
- **Full-text search** — instant fuzzy search across all notes, goals, ideas
- **Daily notes** — automatic daily page as journal/scratch pad
