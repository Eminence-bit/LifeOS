# Vision Beyond v2

The long-term evolution of Life OS. This document exists so that every decision made today stays aligned with where we're going tomorrow.

---

## The Evolution Path

```
Stage 1: Personal Operating System       ← We are here (v1)
    ↓
Stage 2: Collaborative OS                ← Shared Spaces (v2)
    ↓
Stage 3: Life Intelligence Platform      ← AI-native, cross-domain insights (v3)
    ↓
Stage 4: AI Life Companion               ← Proactive, personalised, ambient (v4)
    ↓
Stage 5: Life Ecosystem                  ← Open platform, integrations, API economy
```

---

## Stage 1 — Personal Operating System (v1)

**What it is:** A single unified interface for every life domain. The best personal organiser ever built — not a collection of apps, but an OS.

**Defining characteristic:** Everything in one place, connected by intelligence.

**Success looks like:** A person wakes up, opens Life OS, and has everything they need for the day without opening any other app.

---

## Stage 2 — Collaborative OS (v2)

**What it is:** Personal OS extended to shared spaces — households, couples, families, teams.

**Defining characteristic:** The intelligence of Life OS becomes shared. A household operates as a unit.

**Success looks like:** A family manages grocery, budget, meal planning, chores, and shared calendar together — in one place, with each person's personal data still private.

**New capabilities:** Spaces, members, roles, shared dashboards, voting, chore management, activity feed.

---

## Stage 3 — Life Intelligence Platform (v3)

**What it is:** Life OS stops being a data entry and management tool. It becomes a system that *knows* you deeply enough to surface insights, patterns, and recommendations that you wouldn't discover yourself.

**Defining characteristic:** Cross-domain pattern recognition. The system connects things you don't consciously connect.

**Examples of v3 intelligence:**
- "You sleep better on days you work out before 7pm — your schedule has no workouts this week"
- "Your discretionary spending increases by 40% in weeks you work late — here's a budget buffer"
- "Your German vocabulary retention drops below 60% when you study less than 20 minutes. This week you've studied 8 minutes total"
- "Your body metrics improve most in months where you have a saved goal. You have no active savings goal right now"

**Success looks like:** Life OS tells you something true about yourself that you didn't know.

---

## Stage 4 — AI Life Companion (v4)

**What it is:** Life OS becomes proactive rather than reactive. It doesn't wait for you to open it — it surfaces the right thing at the right moment, across the right surface.

**Defining characteristic:** Ambient intelligence. The system acts like a wise friend who knows everything about your life.

**Examples of v4 behaviour:**
- Morning briefing pushed to your phone before you open the app
- "You have a job interview tomorrow — your Certificate for React expires in 3 days, and your suit is at the dry cleaner per your notes"
- Natural language: "What did I spend on food last month?" → instant answer
- Proactive goals: "Based on your spending and income trend, you could reach your savings goal 3 months early if you reduce dining spend by 15%"

---

## Stage 5 — Life Ecosystem

**What it is:** Life OS opens to the world. An API that allows other apps to read from and write to your life data (with explicit permission). A widget ecosystem. A platform.

**Defining characteristic:** Life OS becomes the authoritative source of truth for a person's life, the way a CRM is the source of truth for a business.

**Examples of Stage 5:**
- A fitness app pushes workout data directly into Health module
- A bank feed automatically categorises expenses
- A browser extension captures articles into Second Brain
- A developer builds a custom "work focus" widget that uses your Planner + Health + Calendar data

---

## Principles That Must Survive All Stages

No matter which stage we're in, these never change:

1. **Privacy by default** — Health, career, and personal data is never shared without explicit user action
2. **Calm over noise** — At every stage, the interface remains focused and uncluttered
3. **Data ownership** — The user always owns their data and can export or delete it
4. **Intelligence is invisible** — AI never announces itself. It just makes the product smarter
5. **Modules stay coherent** — No matter how connected the system becomes, each domain still makes sense on its own
