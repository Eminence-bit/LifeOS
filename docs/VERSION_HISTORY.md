# Version History

A high-level log of Life OS version milestones and their significance.

---

## v1.0 — Personal Operating System

**Started:** 20 July 2026  
**Status:** 🟡 Active Development — Goal: Feature Complete Personal Operating System

The founding version of Life OS. Establishes the single-user personal operating system experience across all nine life domains: Planning, Finance, Food, Health, Learning, Career, Documents, Second Brain, and the Dashboard.

**Defining characteristics:**
- Single user, no collaboration
- Local-first with Supabase cloud sync
- AI intelligence woven into dashboard (Mission, Life Pulse, Momentum, Coach Advice, Predictions, Replay)
- Four theme palettes, dark/light mode
- Gemini AI integration

---

## v1.1 — Polish *(Upcoming)*

**Target:** Post v1.0 feature completion  
**Status:** Planned

Hardening and refinement pass. No new modules. Focus on performance, accessibility, animations, offline resilience, and improved AI insights.

---

## v1.2 — Architecture Evolution *(Upcoming)*

**Target:** After v1.1  
**Status:** Planned

Internal re-architecture to prepare for Version 2. Service layer extraction, event system, realtime improvements, background jobs, caching, and notification service. App behaviour unchanged for users.

---

## v2.0 — Shared Spaces *(Future)*

**Target:** After v1.2  
**Status:** Future

Major platform evolution. Introduces the concept of "Spaces" — shared environments for families, couples, roommates, and teams. Requires a planned database migration from `profile_id` to `space_id`. See `PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md` for full feature list.
